# UKDALE-NILM Dataset Reference

All datasets use **6-second resolution** (UKDALE high-frequency) and contain four appliances:
`dishwasher`, `fridge`, `microwave`, `washing_machine`.

### House 5 Sensor Artefacts

| Appliance | Standby floor | Issue |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| microwave (ch23) | ~48–52 W | Never reads 0 W — a 10 W threshold reads 100% ON. Use `on_power_threshold: 200` from building5.yaml instead. |
| washing_machine / washer_dryer (ch24) | ~14–17 W | Never reads 0 W — a 10 W threshold reads 100% ON. Use `on_power_threshold: 20` from building5.yaml instead. |
| dishwasher (ch22) | 0 W | None — clean channel, matches Houses 1/2. |
| fridge (ch19) | 0 W idle, cycles to ~100–1000+ W | Clean channel; `on_power_threshold: 50` from metadata used for reporting. |

> House 5's real microwave usage is legitimately sparse (short, infrequent zaps) across its
> entire recording — no 44-day window fully escapes this, so activation rates stay low (<0.2%)
> even in the best available window. This reflects real usage, not a selection error.
> CSVs store raw power values; the thresholds above are for activation-rate reporting only.

## Known Issues

- **House 5 standby artefact**: `microwave` and `washing_machine` in all House 5 test splits show `frac_ON = 100%` at low wattage (~32–52 W) when evaluated at a generic 10 W threshold. These appliances never read 0 W. `APR-new-House5-dataset` avoids this by using building5.yaml's own per-appliance `on_power_threshold` values (microwave >200 W, washing_machine >20 W) instead of the generic 10 W threshold; earlier House-5-derived splits (`dataset`, `medium_dataset`, `long_dataset`) still use the generic threshold and are affected.
- **Aggregate baseline shift**: House 5 in August has a ~350–400 W higher aggregate baseline than House 1 in November, likely from summer cooling loads not monitored as individual appliances.
- **House 2 fridge floor**: fridge never reads below 9 W; use a >20 W threshold to detect compressor ON cycles rather than the standard 10 W.
- **House 5 microwave rarity**: even in the cleanest available window, microwave activation stays under 0.2% because real usage in this house is a handful of short zaps — this is genuine sparsity, not a data-selection issue.
- **`APR-new-House1-dataset`, `APR-new-House2-dataset`, and `APR-new-House5-dataset`** are the cleanest options — all splits from the same house, no critical standby artefacts (when using House 5's own thresholds), realistic activation rates.
